Key findings — T_amb = 19°C fixed, both teams:
Aperture: Fully consistent between T6 and T5. 26.67 mm: −13.9% / −13.1% (both under). 10.22–15.25 mm: exactly +24.6% both teams. T6 2.57 mm is the only YES — wide uncertainty (±1.08 × 10⁻⁸) from weak signal, not a model improvement.
Temperature: T6 misses σ at all temperatures. T5 captures σ at 550°C (−1.0%) and 910°C (+0.9%) with a uniform 19°C ambient — these are genuine differences in T5's data, not ambient artifacts. 640°C is the worst segment for both teams (−9.2 to −9.7%). All combined segments miss σ; deviation narrows monotonically from 640°C upward toward 910°C.
Distance: Crossover from over- to under-σ between 20 cm (T6, +5.4%) and 25 cm (T5, −2.8%). Deviation increases beyond 30 cm, consistent with ambient offset dominating at low signal. 45 cm (T5, −19.2%) is anomalously low relative to neighbors — raw data should be verified.
Root cause across all segments: Non-zero intercept B ≈ 0.3–0.7 mW throughout confirms a systematic ambient radiation background not accounted for in the model.
